02 · STRATEGY GUIDE

One market, three lenses

The scanner identifies context before interpreting any signal.

01

Trend Confirmation

Looks for a 20/50-day crossover and confirms it against the 200-day trend. This reduces false calls from small price wiggles.

02

Bollinger + RSI Reversal

Waits for price to leave and then re-enter a Bollinger Band while RSI confirms exhaustion. It does not buy every low RSI reading.

03

ATR Breakout

Uses prior support or resistance, an ATR buffer, and volume confirmation. Today's bar is excluded from the breakout threshold.
